# Tracked implementation knowledge

These instructions apply under `docs/superpowers/`.

- Keep active approved plans in `plans/`, active requirements and designs in `specs/`, and durable decisions in `decisions/`.
- Prefix content filenames with the best-supported `YYMMDD-` creation date. Structural `README.md` and `AGENTS.md` files are exempt.
- Archive useful completed records and update `README.md`; do not leave completed work in active directories.
- Distill maintained conclusions instead of tracking raw transcripts, logs, diffs, screenshots, or repetitive reports.
- Treat all tracked content as public and sanitize credentials, personal data, private topology, and unnecessary production identifiers before commit.
- Do not scan `archive/` during ordinary work. Its local instructions govern selective historical lookup.
